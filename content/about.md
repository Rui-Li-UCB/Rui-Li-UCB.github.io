Hi, I’m Rui. I'm a data engineer in biotech, driven by the belief that better data systems lead to faster scientific breakthroughs. I’m passionate about building scalable and reliable data infrastructure that enables researchers and analysts to focus on discovery.

I specialize in architecting pipelines that turn raw, fragmented, and complex life sciences data into clean, accessible, and interoperable data systems—aligned with industry standards and built to accelerate discovery. Whether supporting clinical studies, automating data validation, or optimizing storage and compute in the cloud, I create solutions that are technically sound and deeply aligned with scientific goals.

In 2023 I graduated from UCB ISchool with a Masters degree in Information Management and Systems(MIMS), focusing on Data Science and Data Engineering. During my time at MIMS I worked alongside product managers, UI/UX designers, data policy researchers, as well as talented engineers. That interdisciplinary experience taught me to understand problems from multiple perspectives — a skill that makes me a stronger engineer and a more thoughtful builder.
